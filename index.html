Charlie · Opening messages from Brazil

Meeting Objective

Understand U.S. weed-resistance lessons and translate them into a more durable Brazilian cotton weed-management strategy.

Key messages I should share first

1. Brazilian cotton weed management is getting more complex.
Caruru / Palmer-type Amaranthus pressure, grasses such as goosegrass, volunteer soybean and complex resistance situations are forcing us to think beyond one POST solution.
2. Our system is highly intensive.
Long growing seasons, tropical conditions, high weed pressure and soybean-cotton rotations create repeated selection pressure across multiple herbicide applications.
3. We are trying to shift from rescue management to preventive management.
One of the biggest advances we are seeing is stronger use of residual herbicides to reduce new flushes and decrease dependence on repeated rescue applications.
4. Durance S is becoming an important tool inside STP.
Durance S is isoxaflutole, an HPPD inhibitor (Group 27), and within STP we are using it as a residual-management tool to strengthen the system and help extend the clean period.
5. The key question for us is durability.
We do not want to replace dependence on one herbicide with dependence on another. We want diversified programs combining traits, residuals and effective POST options.
Positioning: Durance S should be discussed as one component of a broader system. Its strategic value is helping reduce new emergence and reinforce program robustness.
